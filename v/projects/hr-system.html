The brief

Eleven countries, one definition of an employee

A fast-growing services group had acquired six businesses in six years. Each arrival brought its own HRIS, payroll provider and leave policy. Group HR could not produce a reliable headcount on demand, and every month-end payroll close consumed eleven working days across the finance and people teams.

We were engaged to design and deliver a single multi-entity, multi-country HR platform: core employee records, statutory payroll for twelve jurisdictions, time and attendance, an applicant tracking system, performance cycles and an employee self-service portal — plus the integrations that keep finance, IT and operations in sync.

Delivery model: fixed-scope build in five releases over seven months, followed by hypercare and a managed-support retainer. Payroll cutover was staged country-by-country to keep risk contained.

Capabilities

Every HR process, in one governed workspace

Role-based, auditable and configured per country — without forking the codebase.

Unified employee records

One golden record per person with full history, documents, contracts and multi-entity assignments.

Multi-jurisdiction payroll

Twelve statutory rule sets, tax tables, end-of-service calculations and payslip generation per country.

Time & attendance

Shift rosters, biometric and geofenced clock-in, overtime rules and manager approvals in real time.

Recruitment & onboarding

Candidate pipeline, interview scorecards, offer approvals and automated onboarding checklists.

Performance & OKRs

Goal setting, continuous feedback, 360 reviews and calibration sessions with an audit trail.

Learning management

Course catalogue, compliance training, expiry reminders and certification evidence per employee.

Access & approvals

Field-level permissions, four-eyes approvals, SSO via SAML/OIDC and complete change history.

Employee self-service

Leave requests, document requests, payslips and profile updates from web or mobile in 9 languages.

Documents & e-signature

Contract templates, versioned document library and integrated e-sign with tamper-evident certificates.

The employee lifecycle, end to end

Every stage writes to the same record, so payroll, finance and reporting never disagree. Hand-offs are automated; only genuinely human decisions reach a human.

  • Full audit trail on every field change, exportable for auditors
  • Country-specific policy packs applied without code changes

    Technology

    Chosen for auditability and long service life

    HR data is highly regulated and highly relational, so the stack favours strong transactional guarantees, clear ownership boundaries and technology we can hire for in the client's own region.

    Non-negotiables: field-level encryption for national IDs and bank details, immutable audit log, point-in-time recovery, and a documented exit path for every third-party dependency.
