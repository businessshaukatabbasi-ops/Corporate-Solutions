The brief

Three hospitals, one patient record

The group ran a fifteen-year-old HIS at its flagship site, two departmental databases at the others, and paper for everything in between. Bed state, laboratory orders and billing each lived in a different system, so a patient's story had to be re-assembled by hand at every hand-off — and month-end revenue reporting took nine working days to close.

We were engaged to replace the legacy core with a single clinical platform: patient administration and ADT, appointment scheduling, electronic medical records, OPD and IPD management, laboratory and diagnostics, pharmacy and inventory, billing with insurance claims — plus the HL7 and FHIR interfaces that connect imaging, reference labs and payers.

Delivery model: fixed-scope build in six releases over eleven months, cut over site-by-site from the smallest hospital first, then four months of hypercare with two clinical analysts embedded on site during go-live.

Capabilities

Nine clinical modules, one governed record

Role-based, auditable and configured per site — without forking the codebase.

Patient registration & ADT

Front-desk registration, master patient index matching, insurance capture and real-time ADT events to every connected system.

Appointment scheduling

Provider templates, multi-department slot booking, cancellation waitlists and automated reminders across outpatient clinics.

Electronic medical records

Structured notes, vitals, allergies, problem lists and medication history held in one longitudinal chart per patient.

OPD & IPD management

Outpatient queues, ward admissions, bed movement and nursing handover boards with live status at every site.

Laboratory & diagnostics

Order entry, specimen barcoding, analyser interfaces and critical-value alerts with turnaround tracking per test.

Pharmacy & inventory

Ward stock, batch and expiry control, automated reorder levels and dispensing linked to the verified medication order.

Billing, insurance & claims

Charge capture at every clinical touchpoint, tariff rules, payer eligibility checks and claim status through to remittance.

Doctor & nurse dashboards

Role-specific worklists showing today's list, pending orders, discharges and escalations on a single screen.

Reporting & compliance

Census, theatre utilisation, length of stay and revenue reports, with immutable audit logging on every record access.

Workflow

From arrival to discharge, on one record

Every step writes to the same patient chart, so front desk, clinicians, nursing, laboratory and finance never work from a different version of the truth. Routine hand-offs are queued automatically; only clinical judgement reaches a person.

  • Median outpatient check-in cut from 14 minutes to 8.2 minutes
  • ADT events reach connected systems in under 400 ms
  • Every record access is logged and attributable for audit
  • Order sets configured per department without code changes

    Technology

    Built for uptime, privacy and long service life

    Clinical systems are life-critical and heavily regulated, so the stack favours predictable latency, strict auditability and technology the group's own IT team can operate without a vendor on speed dial.

    Non-negotiables: role-based access with break-glass logging, encryption at rest and in transit, 24/7 monitoring with paging, and a documented failover path for every clinical module.
