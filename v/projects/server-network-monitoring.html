The brief

One estate, one honest answer to “is anything broken?”

The operator ran six data centers, 42 field sites and a national transport network on a patchwork of vendor NMS boxes, per-team scripts and spreadsheet checks. Mean time to detect sat at eleven minutes — when an alert fired at all. Roughly two thirds of the tickets that did arrive were noise, so engineers had learned to treat the pager as background static.

We were engaged to design and deliver a single observability platform for the whole estate: agent and agentless discovery, server, container and network metrics, flow analysis, dependency topology, correlation-aware alerting with escalation policies, SLA reporting, and an automation layer that turns the repeat incidents into runbooks the NOC can execute unattended.

Delivery model: phased rollout over six months — discovery and collectors first, then alerting and escalation, then automation — followed by a 24/7 managed service with quarterly alert-tuning reviews.

Capabilities

Everything the NOC watches, in one console

Server, network, flow and application telemetry collected under one model — so every alert arrives with its topology, its owner and its SLA attached.

Agentless & agent discovery

Scheduled SNMP sweeps plus a lightweight agent where needed — new hosts are found, classified and baselined without raising a ticket.

Server & container metrics

CPU, memory, disk, network, process and per-container statistics from Linux, Windows and Kubernetes, scraped at 15-second resolution.

Network flow & bandwidth

NetFlow and sFlow records resolved to talkers, applications and sites, so bandwidth arguments end with evidence instead of opinion.

Topology & dependency maps

Layer-2 and layer-3 neighbour data builds the live map, then services are attached so impact can be traced across the whole chain.

Alerting with escalation policies

Deduplication, dependency suppression and business-hours aware routing to on-call rosters, with timed escalation to the duty manager.

SLA & uptime reporting

Per-service, per-site and per-month availability with error budgets, breach history and exportable reports for customers and the board.

Log aggregation & search

Syslog, Windows event and application logs indexed beside the metrics, with 90-day hot retention and full-text search across the estate.

Automated remediation runbooks

Guard-railed actions — restart a service, drain a node, clear a queue — triggered from the alert and written to an immutable audit log.

Asset inventory & CMDB

Serials, warranties, firmware, rack positions and owners maintained automatically from discovery and the collector fleet.

Workflow

From first sweep to unattended remediation

Observability is a loop, not a project. Discovery feeds the model, the model feeds correlation, and every incident that gets reviewed makes the next alert sharper than the last.

  • Detection-to-alert median held at 40 seconds across the estate
  • Every alert carries an owner, a runbook link and an escalation policy
  • Change and maintenance windows suppress noise before it pages anyone
  • Quarterly tuning retires alerts nobody has acted on in 90 days
01

Discover & baseline

An SNMP and credential sweep maps every routable device, agentless host and flow exporter. Each node is given a 14-day behavioural baseline, so thresholds start from reality rather than vendor defaults.

SNMP sweepAgent rolloutBaselines
02

Model thresholds & health rules

Static limits are replaced with dynamic thresholds per device class, and health rules that combine CPU, disk, error counters and dependency state into one honest service score.

Dynamic thresholdsHealth rulesDevice classes
03

Detect & correlate

The pipeline evaluates 1.2 million series continuously, then collapses related symptoms against the topology map — one flapping uplink must never arrive as forty separate server alerts.

1.2M seriesEvent correlationDedup
04

Alert & escalate

Severity drives the route: chat and ticket for warnings, pager and voice bridge for critical. Unacknowledged incidents escalate along a timed path to the duty manager.

On-call rotaPager routingEscalation
05

Remediate & runbook

Cleared repeat incidents become approved runbooks: restart, scale, fail over or quarantine — executed with a dry-run preview, a guard rail and a full action log.

RunbooksGuard railsAction log
06

Review & improve

Monthly incident reviews and quarterly tuning sessions retire dead alerts, tighten thresholds and extend coverage where the estate grew — findings feed straight back to step one.

Technology

Chosen for scale, retention and no vendor lock-in

The estate produces roughly 400,000 samples per second, so the stack favours write-friendly time-series storage, open collection standards, and components the client's own team can operate without us in the loop.

Non-negotiables: SNMP v3 with encrypted communities, 13 months of hot metrics plus 24 months cold, a Prometheus-compatible query surface, and a tested exit path — every dashboard can be rebuilt from raw data on open tooling.
