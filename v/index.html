A process that removes surprises

Five disciplined stages, fixed checkpoints and transparent reporting from kickoff to continuous improvement.

STEP 01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, process mapping, current-state audit and a prioritised requirements backlog.

STEP 02

Design

Information architecture, UX flows, design system, technical blueprint and a signed-off delivery plan.

STEP 03

Build

Two-week sprints, automated CI, code review gates and a demo environment you can touch from day one.

STEP 04

Deploy

Data migration, UAT, cutover rehearsal, go-live runway and hypercare with war-room coverage.

STEP 05

Support

SLA-backed monitoring, patching, quarterly roadmap reviews and measured continuous improvement.
